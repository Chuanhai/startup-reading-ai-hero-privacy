# Privacy Policy for Reading AI Hero

**Last Updated: October 20, 2026**

Reading AI Hero ("the App") helps parents and families turn their own printed books into interactive read-aloud experiences.

We respect your privacy and aim to process your reading content locally on your device.

## 1. Information You Provide

The App allows you to take photographs of book pages or select images from your photo library.

Images, recognized text, reading progress, and generated audio are stored locally on your device. These materials are not uploaded to our servers for text recognition or speech generation.

## 2. On-Device Text Recognition and Speech

The App uses on-device text recognition to identify text in selected book images.

The App also uses an offline text-to-speech model to generate spoken audio locally on your device. Your book images and recognized text are not sent to an external AI service for these functions.

## 3. Purchases and Subscriptions

Reading AI Hero offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions through Apple's App Store.

Apple processes payments and manages subscription transactions. The App uses Apple's StoreKit framework to verify subscription status and determine access to paid features.

We do not collect or store your payment card information.

## 4. Free Page Allowance

The App may store a local record of your monthly free-page usage in your device's Keychain.

The App may contact a remote website over HTTPS to verify the current month for free-page allowance management. This request may disclose standard network information, such as your IP address, to the website operator.

Your book images and recognized text are not included in this request.

## 5. Data Storage and Retention

Book images, recognized text, reading progress, and generated speech data are stored locally on your device.

You may delete saved books within the App. Uninstalling the App generally removes its locally stored documents and audio data, although certain Keychain records may persist according to iOS behavior.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Reading AI Hero is intended to help parents and caregivers support family reading activities.

The App does not require children to create accounts or provide their names, email addresses, or other personal information.

## 7. Third-Party Services

The App uses Apple services for app distribution, purchases, and subscription verification.

The App may make an HTTPS request to retrieve trusted date information for monthly free-page tracking.

These third-party services may process technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

## 8. Security

We use on-device processing and system-provided storage mechanisms to reduce unnecessary transmission of reading content.

No electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's functionality or data practices change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised date.
