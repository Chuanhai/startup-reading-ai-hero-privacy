# Reading AI Hero — Support

Welcome to Reading AI Hero Support!

Reading AI Hero helps parents and caregivers turn printed English picture books into interactive read-aloud experiences using on-device text recognition and offline speech generation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I add a book?**

Open the app, take photos of book pages or import images from your photo library, and follow the instructions to recognize the text.

**Can I use the app offline?**

Text recognition and speech generation work on your device once the required speech model is downloaded. Some functions, including subscriptions and initial downloads, may require an internet connection.

**How many pages can I use for free?**

You can import up to 3 new book pages each month for free.

**What does the subscription include?**

Unlimited Reading allows unlimited new page imports for $2.99 per month in the United States. Prices may vary by region.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**

Open your iPhone Settings, tap your Apple Account, select Subscriptions, and choose Reading AI Hero.

**How do I restore my subscription?**

Open the subscription page in Reading AI Hero and tap Restore Purchases.
